Questions about Macau

Why does Macau have no published resilience score?

Macau reaches 61% coverage. WHO, Reporters Without Borders, and the World Bank debt feed do not supply all standalone SAR observations needed to reach the 65% floor because provider systems classify the SAR inconsistently.

What evidence is available for Macau?

It contains 14 observed dimension readings, including Import concentration, State continuity, and Liquid-reserve adequacy. The values remain evidence components and are not a published Macau rank.
